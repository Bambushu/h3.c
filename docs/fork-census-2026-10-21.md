## What the measurements agree on

- **Attention dominates at production shapes.** Issue #13: 1344x768/124f = 37,296 rows, denoise
  36 min on M5 Max, wall = MPSGraph wait. KaedeTai: attention is 60% of denoise at 107-175 frames,
  runs at 22-46 TFLOP/s where the linears reach 92. danmo-ai: attention is 76-99% of host encode.
- **A faster DENSE kernel does not exist on Apple.** qpzmV's hand-written NAX SDPA: 23-46x slower
  than MPSGraph. danmo-ai's streamed Flash: 12x slower at 1344x768. KaedeTai's port of MLX steel
  attention: parity with MPSGraph, near the bf16 roofline at head_dim 128. int8 attention
  (Sage-style) buys 10% on QK and nothing on PV (KaedeTai bench_qk).
- **Masking inside MPSGraph cannot do sparsity** (yangweijie F39): MPSGraph packs token rows shared
  by all 56 heads, so top-k selection cannot land; net negative.
- **Therefore the only attention lever is a sparse custom kernel** (SolAttn / Veda / VSA) or a
  distilled model. Sparse kernels for h3c exist (below).
- Apple's SDPA error jumps 10-15x above N≈9,100 rows (neokree float64 oracle); query-blocking
  restores the bf16 floor. All of Mike's 1 MP renders sit above that line. Unverified by us.
- Determinism is disputed: neokree sees run-to-run byte differences at 640x352/124f; JunzoKamahara
  could not reproduce on M4 Max or M5 in any controlled test.

## Independent engines to benchmark against

- **tgo-app-dev/vpipe** (218 stars, Apache 2.0, C++/Metal): 960x544/124f/6 steps exact mode on an
  M5 Pro 24 GB: 5:29 vs h3.c 7:11. Renders fur without h3.c's mosaic (issue #70).
- **MacOS-H3-Speedrun** (EvolvingLMMs-Lab) and BSAI Sol-H3: ComfyUI stacks with Turbo + SolAttn,
  the local ComfyUI lane's reference numbers.
- **FastVideo/FastH3**: 4-step VSA and 8-step V2 distillations (700k+ downloads). Native h3c support
  in AlexanderIstomin/fzeng656. A turbo lane, parked.
