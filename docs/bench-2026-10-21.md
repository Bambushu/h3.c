# Bench, 2026-10-21: upstream vs this fork on an M5 Pro 48 GB

Machine: MacBook Pro M5 Pro, 48 GB, macOS 27.0, Metal 4, `--ssd-streaming` (the
original BF16 DiT streamed from the internal SSD, 0.72 GiB/s). Model: the released
FL2VA snapshot. Reference shot: `prompt_vertical.txt` (a woman speaking one line to
camera), 124 frames, 8 steps, 50 blocks, reuse 1, seed 77001, `H3_VAE_TILE_PIXELS=256`.
Every number is one run; wall seconds from `--profile`. Raw logs, frames and clips:
`~/h3c-bench/`. Whisper (large-v3-turbo) transcribed the spoken line verbatim in
every run below, and the stationary-grid score stayed at 1.07 to 1.22 (visible grid
starts around 1.5).

## What the numbers say

- **bf16 video VAE is the lane.** 3.8x faster decode at 0.5 MP, 3.2x at 1 MP, 63 dB
  against F32 on identical latents. Prefetch and cache are bit-identical by
  construction (jerryfane's measurement); the pixels differ only through bf16.
- **The integration build is not bit-identical to upstream at the same seed.** The
  text-encoder GQA race fix (#63) is the one merged change documented as
  output-changing; unverified which commit moved the take. Speech and grid unchanged.
- **Sol-Attn works and is sparse** (6-10% of blocks routed at tau 1.3), but the
  denoise only drops 8% at 0.5 MP and 15% at 1 MP. Working back from the per-step
  times, attention is about 27% of a dense step at 0.5 MP and 35% at 1 MP on this
  lane; the bf16 linears streamed from SSD are the other two thirds. The sparse
  kernel measured 12.7x (19k rows) and 22.6x (37k rows) faster than dense MPSGraph
  attention in isolation (`h3_sol_attention_bench`, random inputs), so the
  attention share is what caps the gain.
- **Sol output is a different take, not a degraded one**, at 20 dB from dense.
  KaedeTai measured the same effect for a dense kernel swap (26 dB): bf16
  accumulation order amplified over the steps. A fixed seed does not survive a
  kernel change; judge Sol takes on their own.
- **Apple's SDPA is not degraded above 9.1k rows here.** `sdpa_probe` at 4k to
  18.9k rows, 56 heads: rel-L2 1.7e-3 against float64 for whole-sequence and
  query-blocked alike, every encode bit-reproducible. neokree's cliff does not
  transfer to this machine, so PR #64's split stays opt-in.

## Next levers, in order

1. **Int8 weights instead of streamed BF16.** Two thirds of a step is linear
   layers running BF16 from SSD. The resident int8 path (NAX TensorOps on M5:
   KaedeTai measured 92 TFLOP/s int8 against 58 bf16) needs about 33 GB for the
   DiT, tight on 48 GB with the VAE prefetched; PR #55's pre-quantized int8 cache
   streams per block at a 2 to 5 GiB peak. Port #55 and measure.
2. **Sol on more steps.** `H3_SOL_ATTN_ALL_STEPS=1` or a 10%-100% window; yshenaw
   keeps only the first step dense.
3. **Morton ordering of the video rows** (yshenaw's `_morton_h3.py`) so 64-token
   blocks are spatially compact; expected to route fewer blocks at the same tau.
